## Navigation

- **Header:** STRIPED tee logo · shop · the world · about · **join ↗** (the action, in red).
- **Phone:** logo · join · a "menu" stripe that opens into a full blue panel. The menu literally opens as a stripe.

## The visitor's path (primary)

hero (understands: clothes + music + LA, in about 3 s) → the pieces (sees the real product and price) → before → after (why it costs what it costs) → patch draw (plays) → music (feels the world) → join or shop.
